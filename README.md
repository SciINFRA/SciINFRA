# SciINFRA

## Independent Scientific Infrastructure for AI Research and Development

SciINFRA is an independent research and development infrastructure for artificial intelligence, scientific computing, model evaluation, dataset engineering, controlled sandbox environments, and governed agentic intelligence.

### Mission

To build and operate the environments, tools, datasets, protocols, and evaluation systems required to research, develop, train, test, and improve intelligent systems.

### Research and Development Domains

- Scientific computing and computational research
- Machine learning and AI development
- Dataset engineering and data production
- Controlled research environments and sandboxes
- Model testing, evaluation, and benchmarking
- Agentic systems and multi-agent research
- Governed intelligence architectures
- Reproducible experiments and research artifacts

### Core Research Protocols

- MetaGate / Genesis: governed intelligence and authorization architecture.
- GHOST: procedural research environments, sandbox specifications, and benchmark systems.

### Operating Model

SciINFRA develops infrastructure and research artifacts that support the AI production and research lifecycle:

1. Build environments, tools, datasets, and infrastructure.
2. Conduct experiments and model development.
3. Train, test, and evaluate intelligent systems.
4. Generate datasets, benchmarks, code, and research artifacts.
5. Feed validated results into further research and development.

### Organizational Scope

SciINFRA operates within the Digital Genesis organizational ecosystem.

Its scope is scientific infrastructure, AI, machine learning, research environments, and computational systems. Sonic engineering, audio DSP, and music technology remain separate domains.
