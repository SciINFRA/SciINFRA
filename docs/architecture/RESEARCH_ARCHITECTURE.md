# SciINFRA Research Architecture

## 1. Research Framework

SciINFRA operates as an integrated scientific research and development environment. Its domains are independently organized but can exchange validated data, tools, experimental results, and research artifacts.

## 2. Research Domains

### 2.1 Scientific Computing
Development and application of computational methods, numerical systems, scientific programming, and reproducible computational workflows.

### 2.2 Machine Learning and AI
Research into model architectures, training methodologies, inference systems, optimization, and independent AI development.

### 2.3 Dataset Engineering
Design, generation, transformation, documentation, validation, and provenance management of datasets for AI research and evaluation.

### 2.4 Research Environments and Sandboxes
Construction of controlled computational environments for experimentation, simulation, agent development, containment, and behavioral observation.

### 2.5 Model Evaluation and Benchmarking
Development of evaluation protocols, reproducible benchmarks, performance measurements, behavioral tests, and comparative research.

### 2.6 Agentic Systems
Research into autonomous agents, multi-agent coordination, emergent behavior, agent communication, control structures, and human-in-the-loop systems.

### 2.7 Governed Intelligence
Research into authorization, coherence, state transitions, ethical constraints, consequence evaluation, and governance architectures.

## 3. Cross-Domain Integration

Research domains may exchange:

- Validated datasets
- Computational tools
- Experimental environments
- Benchmark specifications
- Model evaluation results
- Research code
- Technical documentation

Integration must preserve the provenance and original ownership of each artifact.

## 4. Protocol Separation

### MetaGate / Genesis
Maintains its own conceptual and technical research boundary within governed intelligence architecture.

### GHOST
Maintains its own research boundary within procedural environments, sandbox systems, state machines, and benchmark development.

These protocol families may interact with other SciINFRA research domains through explicitly defined interfaces. They are not interchangeable projects.

## 5. Research Integrity

All research activities should support:

1. Reproducibility
2. Traceable provenance
3. Documented assumptions
4. Explicit evaluation criteria
5. Controlled experimentation
6. Clear separation between hypotheses and validated findings

## 6. Research Lifecycle

Research progresses through:

1. Problem definition
2. Conceptual specification
3. Environment and tool development
4. Experimentation
5. Data collection
6. Evaluation
7. Validation
8. Documentation and artifact publication

## 7. Long-Term Objective

Establish SciINFRA as an independent scientific infrastructure capable of producing research environments, datasets, computational systems, benchmarks, and validated technical artifacts that contribute to the broader AI research and development ecosystem.
