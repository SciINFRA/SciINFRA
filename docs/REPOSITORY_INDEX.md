**Repository:** SciINFRA  
**Organization:** SciINFRA

## 1. Overview

SciINFRA is an independent scientific and AI research infrastructure ecosystem.

This repository organizes its research architecture, computational infrastructure, experimental environments, datasets, evaluation systems, and specialized research protocols.

It is structured to support the complete research and development lifecycle, from infrastructure design and dataset production through experimentation, model evaluation, and validated research outputs.

## 5. Research and Development Lifecycle

SciINFRA operates through a connected research lifecycle:

1. Infrastructure development
2. Research environment construction
3. Dataset engineering and production
4. Model and agent experimentation
5. Benchmark execution
6. Model evaluation
7. Analysis and validation
8. Research artifact production
9. Integration of findings into subsequent development

## 6. Research Artifact Standards

Research artifacts should preserve:

- Unique identifiers and version information
- Source and provenance
- Relevant configurations and dependencies
- Execution records
- Results and supporting evidence
- Licensing and distribution restrictions
- Validation and implementation status

Specifications, prototypes, executed experiments, and validated systems must be distinguished.

## 7. Organizational Scope

SciINFRA operates within the Digital Genesis organizational ecosystem.

Its scope includes scientific computing, AI and ML research, data engineering, controlled environments, evaluation, and governed agentic intelligence.

Vajrayana Sound Lab remains a separate sonic engineering and DSP research organization. Audio technology and music production are outside the scope of this repository.

## 8. Development Status

This repository is the foundational documentation and architecture layer for SciINFRA.

The existence of a specification does not establish that its proposed system has been implemented or operationally validated.

Future development will progressively add executable tools, datasets, experimental environments, benchmarks, and validated research artifacts.
