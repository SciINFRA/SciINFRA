# SciINFRA — Project Overview

## 1. Purpose

SciINFRA is an independent scientific infrastructure laboratory focused on the development of AI research environments, computational tools, datasets, evaluation systems, and governed intelligent architectures.

## 2. Infrastructure Lifecycle

SciINFRA participates across the AI research and production lifecycle:

- Infrastructure development
- Research environment construction
- Dataset engineering and generation
- Model training and experimentation
- Testing and evaluation
- Benchmark development
- Research artifact production
- Iterative system improvement

## 3. Primary Domains

### Scientific Computing
Computational research, numerical methods, scientific programming, and reproducible workflows.

### AI and Machine Learning
Model development, training environments, experimentation, and independent research.

### Data Engineering
Dataset specifications, data production, documentation, and provenance.

### Research Environments
Sandbox construction, simulation, controlled experimentation, and containment research.

### Evaluation
Benchmarking, performance measurement, behavioral evaluation, and reproducibility.

### Governed Intelligence
Research into authorization, coherence, control, and consequence-aware intelligent systems.

## 4. Core Protocols

MetaGate / Genesis and GHOST are distinct research protocol families maintained within SciINFRA.

## 5. Deliverables

SciINFRA produces:

- Research code
- Computational tools
- Datasets
- Experimental environments
- Benchmarks
- Evaluation protocols
- Technical documentation
- Research findings and reproducible artifacts

## 6. Development Principle

Infrastructure, research, and evaluation should be independently documented, reproducible, and extensible.
